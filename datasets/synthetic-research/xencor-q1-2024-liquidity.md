# Xencor Q1 2024: a deep current-asset cushion

Xencor, Inc. filed its quarterly report for the period ended March 31, 2024. Figures below are in thousands of US dollars, as presented by the company.

Xencor reported total current assets of $562,252 thousand at March 31, 2024. Total current liabilities were $79,402 thousand.

That leaves working capital of $482,850 thousand and a current ratio of 7.08.

The current-asset position comfortably exceeds near-term obligations.
