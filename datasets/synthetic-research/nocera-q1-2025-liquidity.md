# Nocera Q1 2025: liquidity covers near-term obligations

Nocera, Inc. filed its first-quarter report for the period ended March 31, 2025. This note looks only at the balance sheet and short-term liquidity.

Nocera reported total current assets of $1,275,416 at March 31, 2025. Total current liabilities were $706,384 on the same date.

That leaves working capital of $569,032 and a current ratio of 1.81.

| Measure | March 31, 2025 |
| --- | --- |
| Total current assets | $1,275,416 |
| Total current liabilities | $706,384 |
| Working capital | $569,032 |

On these figures, current assets cover short-term obligations with room to spare, and the balance sheet does not point to an immediate need for new financing.
